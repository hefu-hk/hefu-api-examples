# AI Tools Workspace

The [HeFu AI Tools Workspace](https://hefu.hk/tools) provides task-oriented, no-code interfaces for people who do not need to construct API requests themselves.

## Choose a task

- [Marketing Studio](https://hefu.hk/tools/studio) — marketing content drafting.
- [Seller Assistant](https://hefu.hk/tools/listing) — cross-border product listings.
- [HR Assistant](https://hefu.hk/tools/hr) — recruiting content.
- [Developer Toolkit](https://hefu.hk/tools/devkit) — developer-oriented tasks.
- [Creative Studio](https://hefu.hk/tools/creator) — visual creative work.
- [Support Widget](https://hefu.hk/tools/widget) — site-support workflows.

Product availability and controls can change; the current tool page is authoritative.

## Suggested workflow

1. Open the tool that matches your task and sign in with your HeFu account.
2. Provide real source material, such as product attributes or a support question, rather than asking for generic copy.
3. Review factual accuracy, language, brand voice, and any claims before using the output.
4. Check the workspace consumption and your HeFu call records or wallet details for actual usage.
5. Move to the [unified API quickstart](../quickstart/python_openai_sdk.py) only when you need custom orchestration, application integration, or repeatable programmatic output.

For local-language customer content, review output with a person who understands the target market. Do not treat generated claims, translations, or compliance statements as publication-ready without review.
